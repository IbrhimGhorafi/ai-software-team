# Identify threats and prioritize mitigations

## When to use
Use this skill when the task involves identify threats and prioritize mitigations.

## Procedure
Map assets, trust boundaries, actors, entry points, and sensitive flows. Consider spoofing, tampering, repudiation, information disclosure, denial of service, and privilege escalation. Rank risks and propose practical mitigations.

## Quality checks
- Confirm scope, assumptions, and relevant constraints.
- Follow the shared instructions in `.claude/CLAUDE.md` and applicable files under `standards/`.
- Prefer small, reversible changes and preserve traceability to requirements.

- Identify risks, open questions, and next actions explicitly.
