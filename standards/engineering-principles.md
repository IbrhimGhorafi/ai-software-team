# Engineering Principles

- Optimize for user value, clarity, maintainability, and operability.
- Inspect before changing; make small, reversible changes.
- Do not choose project technologies before requirements and constraints are known.
- Prefer explicit interfaces, clear errors, testable modules, and observable behavior.
- Avoid arbitrary test ratios; use risk, cost of failure, and feedback speed.
- Keep project-specific decisions in ADRs and project docs, not global standards.
- Do not claim tests passed unless they were executed and observed.
- Use dependency pinning and update policies appropriate to the project.
